Concept of Pool is to have limited 3 heroes per role. Typically they are strong heroes that are generally regarded to be suited to the role. 

Heroes in pool should fill a [[Role]] and ideally all fit into the same style, in order to ease playing similarly between matches.

There can be flavor of the month builds but they should not be included in the pool as they are unlikely to be good for extended periods. Goal is to gain mastery in limited amount of heroes and expand there.

Example of a pool for solo ranked grinding would be:
* [[Treant Protector]]
* [[Lich]]
* [[CM]]

Despite the heroes being very different they offer somewhat similar capabilities as all are:
* Strong laners
* Counter initiation/save
* Not item dependant
