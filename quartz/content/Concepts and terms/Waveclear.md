Ability to clear waves 
1. Quickly 
2. Safely 

This is done in order to both get a lot of gold and XP and then move on to farming jungle and additionally to push waves and get better [[Map Control]].

After the laning has ended if you don't have waveclear then don't go there lasthitting as a support, as you'll be likely jumped.

Examples:
Bane pre shard > No waveclear, will get jumped
Treant > Nature's grasp waveclear is safe from invisibility
